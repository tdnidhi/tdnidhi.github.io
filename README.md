## Selected work

**Sophos credit memo and model (private credit)**
A lender's assessment of the Thoma Bravo take-private, updated for its October 2026 refinancing. It covers leverage and coverage on three EBITDA bases, operating scenarios, a first lien recovery waterfall and Pluralsight and Medallia precedents. Recommendation: avoid new exposure, monitor.
Files: `Sophos_Credit_Memo.pdf`, `Sophos_Model.xlsx`

**Chevron (CVX) 10-year DCF valuation**
Excel DCF built on Bloomberg data, with a CAPM-based WACC of 9.11%, Gordon Growth terminal value, worst/base/best oil-price scenarios and a WACC by terminal growth sensitivity table.
File: `Chevron_DCF_Report.pdf`

**Oil vs. S&P 500 Event Analyzer (Python)**
Analysis of oil and equity relationships across 2,828 trading days, 26 stocks and 8 geopolitical event windows, built as a modular Python package with the regressions written in NumPy.
Code: https://github.com/tdnidhi/Oil_SPX_Analyzer

**IBKR portfolio simulation**
Head of Research for a $1M simulated portfolio: equity research across 10+ securities, DCF valuations and beta-adjusted positioning. Return of 6.36% over 3 months.
