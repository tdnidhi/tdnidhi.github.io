Sophos credit memo

Personal project · Lender’s view of the Thoma Bravo take-private · Updated Nov 2026 · Read the memo (PDF) ↗ · Download the model (Excel)

9.5xPro forma leverage on cash-basis EBITDA, up from 6.3x in FY2025
1.15xFY2026E EBITDA to interest coverage after the refinancing
86–100¢First lien recovery across the distress waterfall
Avoid newRecommendation: monitor existing positions

After a failed $2.5B private credit refinancing in May 2026, Sophos closed a refinancing in October 2026 with a GTCR-led $200mm second lien and a reportedly extended 2029 first lien. The memo asks whether a direct lender should add exposure and concludes that the last known 96.5¢ price sits about 10.5¢ above the 86¢ low-case recovery, with thin coverage and negative free cash flow. The closed terms are not public, so the memo flags spreads and maturities as proposed or assumed.

Pro forma debt of about $2,743mm. Only the marketed basis clears the 2.0x coverage line, so covenant headroom depends on how the credit agreement defines EBITDA.

What would change the view

  • Confirmation of the closed terms and the governing EBITDA definition.
  • Cash-basis EBITDA above about $375mm (about 1.5x coverage).
  • Status of the December 2026 revolver, still unconfirmed.
  • A secondary price below about 90¢ if the 2029 maturity and pricing are confirmed.

Oil vs. S&P 500 analyzer

Coursework · Python, Bloomberg and Yahoo Finance · Dec 2014 – Mar 2026 · Code on GitHub ↗

5.4%Of daily S&P 500 variation explained by oil (R² = 0.054)
−0.50Oil to S&P 500 correlation in the 2026 Iran war
+41% / −5%Oil vs S&P 500, Feb–Mar 2026
17.4%Of trading days with an oil move above 3%

Oil explains little of the market on average, but a geopolitical supply shock reverses the relationship and splits sectors in opposite directions. A demand shock like COVID does not. Built as a five-module Python package, with the regressions written from scratch in NumPy.

Pure oil beta by stock, controlling for S&P 500 and VIX

26 stocks, six sectors. Energy is strongly positive, airlines negative.
